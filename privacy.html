Bow Blast: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Bow Blast is a game by Danilo Fiocco, for iPhone and the web (danilofiocco.github.io/balloonGame). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information the game stores on your device

Your saved scores, your last leaderboard entry and which arrow tutorials you have seen are kept on your device only (in the game's local storage). Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, removes them.

Leaderboard

If you choose to save a score, the name you type (up to 9 characters) and your score are sent to our leaderboard and shown publicly to all players. Please don't use your real name or anything personal as your leaderboard name. The leaderboard is stored with Supabase.

Anonymous play statistics

To understand how the game is played and balance it, the game sends anonymous game statistics such as shots fired, balloons popped, coins earned and spent, bosses defeated, how long a game lasted and the game version. Each game gets a random identifier that is not linked to you, your device or your leaderboard name. These statistics are also stored with Supabase.

Advertising (iPhone app)

The iPhone app shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad when you play again after a game over, and an optional ad you can choose to watch for extra arrows. Google may collect and use information from your device, such as its advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Learn how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and read Google's Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with PRIVACY in the game's menu. The web version of the game does not show ads.

Children

Bow Blast is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

What we don't do

We don't ask for an account, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and we don't sell your information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: open an issue at github.com/danilofiocco/balloonGame/issues.
